Privacy policy

CSP · 6 November 2026

CSP is a private test app. When you sign in with Google, it can access only what you allow on Google's consent screen, and uses it only to move your own files between your device and your Google Drive when you ask it to.

  • Nothing is collected, kept on any server, shared or sold.
  • Your sign-in stays on your own device. You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
